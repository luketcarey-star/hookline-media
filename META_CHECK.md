# Meta check: run on EVERY post before it goes to Buffer (Luke's pages are monetized)

Check the finished post as people will see it: every rendered slide (read the JPGs), the caption,
the hashtags and the alt text. Also load the `meta-posting-rules` skill if it's available and apply
it. A post goes to Buffer only if every line below is a clear YES. If anything fails, fix it and
check again; if it can't be fixed, replace the post with a different topic. Never "probably fine".

## Community Standards (could get the page restricted)
- [ ] No violence, injuries, blood, bodies or deaths, in words or images. No fights, chases,
      shootings or rough arrests (arrests shown, if at all, are calm).
- [ ] No real person's likeness in AI images; no imitation bodycam frames, mugshots, police
      badges or logos, court documents, news photos or brand logos.
- [ ] No minors connected to any allegation, crime or dispute.
- [ ] Nobody targeted: no home addresses, phone numbers, badge or ID numbers, no "go after /
      call / expose this officer, neighbour or official". Private people named only if the case
      was widely reported.
- [ ] Allegations stay allegations ("accused", "the lawsuit claims") unless there was a ruling.
- [ ] No hate, slurs, insults about groups, profanity or sexual content. Nothing framed around race,
      religion or immigration.
- [ ] Every real fact (names, dates, amounts, rulings, laws) was confirmed in this run's fact-check.
      No invented cases, quotes or laws. No general legal advice ("your rights are...").

## Monetization policies (could lose monetization)
- [ ] No engagement bait: no "comment / vote / like / share / tag / type X". An open question is fine.
- [ ] No clickbait: no "you won't believe", no all-caps sentences, no "!!!", the hook matches the post.
- [ ] Calm, factual tone. Not sensational, not shocking, not ragebait or outrage-farming.
- [ ] Neutral on politics, elections and other debated social issues: no campaigning for or against
      police, parties or causes; the post reports what happened and what the court decided.
- [ ] Original: our own Hookline slides and images, nothing copied from other pages, no reposted
      photos or clips.
- [ ] Not misleading: images match the story (generic stand-ins, nothing presented as a real photo of
      the real event).

## Record it
Save `"metaCheck": "passed"` (and a few words on anything you fixed) on each post in history. List
any post you fixed or replaced in the run summary. If a post had to be dropped, that's a
`⚠️ PROBLEM` line.

# What works on Jury Juice (the bot keeps this up to date from Buffer insights)

Last updated: 2026-10-09 am (15 measured posts, Oct 5 to 8; numbers still early and small)
Engagement rate = (reactions + comments + shares + saves) / reach (FB: impressions).

## Do more
- Police, HOA and county / city government stories (Luke's rule; police most).
- Single photo posts: 4 measured averaged ~1,280 FB impressions (Brake Light 3,157; Drug Dog 1,398;
  Venice 304; guinea pigs 276) vs ~200 for 9 tweet-style posts.
- Real-case police rulings told as one clear story (Heien: 20 comments, 2 shares; Rodriguez:
  20 reactions, 2 shares). Only 2 posts so far: keep testing, but it's the strongest signal yet.
- Short captions: one punchy line + a question (Luke's rule; Facebook flagged long captions).

## Do less
- Carousels: 3 measured, FB reach 70 to 228 and 0 to 0.4% engagement, IG 0% on all 3.
- Quirky non-US / general novelty topics (Venice pigeons, guinea pigs, weevils, monkey selfie,
  terms & conditions joke): 6 posts, 0 to 1% FB engagement, 0 reactions on most.
- Long, detailed captions or source lists.

## Test next
- Quick questions: 2 measured (jury duty 2 comments; HOA letter 0). Try sharper, more personal
  police / ticket questions to see if any land.
- Tweet backgrounds: light / black / dim each have only 1 to 2 posts, all near 0%. No winner yet.
- Instagram reach is low everywhere (22 to 174); photo singles do best there too (1.6 to 1.9%).
- Closing questions: "was the court right?" vs "would you have...?" on police rulings.
